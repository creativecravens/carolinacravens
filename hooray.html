Brand Process · Design Thinking

Every project starts with
a brand foundation.

One of the first things I do when joining a new company or project is study and document the brand. I created these brand guidelines as a personal reference tool while preparing for this opportunity with Hooray Health. This is how I work: before I design a single asset, I make sure I deeply understand the visual language, color system, typography, and design principles of the organization I'm creating for.

At every role I've held, I have built or refined brand guidelines that ensure visual consistency across every touchpoint — from member-facing materials and benefit explainers to marketing campaigns and digital assets. For Hooray Health, a company whose brand promise is to feel warm, honest, and unmistakably human, that consistency is everything.

Brand Guidelines · Colors & Typography
Documented Hooray Health's complete color system (Hooray Purple, Deep Plum, Soft Lavender, Spark Amber, Fresh Green) and typography pairing (Manrope for display, DM Sans for body) as a working reference for consistent design execution.
Brand System · Color Ratios & Applications
Analyzed Hooray Health's brand hierarchy: 50% Purple, 25% Plum, 15% Lavender, 7% Green, 3% Amber. Mapped brand applications across member dashboard, provider search, benefit explainers, welcome letters, and marketing landing pages.

"Purple does the heavy lifting; deep plum supports it. Amber is a punctuation mark, never a sentence. Lavender is a whisper, used for hierarchy on dark surfaces." — This is the level of brand thinking I bring to every project, every day.
