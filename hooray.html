I created these brand guidelines as a personal reference tool while preparing for this opportunity with Hooray Health. This is how I work: before I design a single asset, I make sure I deeply understand the visual language, color system, typography, and design principles of the organization I'm creating for.

At every role I've held, I have built or refined brand guidelines that ensure visual consistency across every touchpoint from member-facing materials and benefit explainers to marketing campaigns and digital assets. Brand Application

Brand System
Color Ratios & Use

Analyzed Hooray Health's brand hierarchy: 50% Purple does the heavy lifting, 25% Plum supports it, 15% Lavender whispers on dark surfaces, 7% Green for wellness, 3% Amber as punctuation. Mapped applications across member dashboard, benefit explainers, welcome letters, and marketing landing pages.

Color Ratios Brand in Use Applications

"Purple does the heavy lifting; deep plum supports it. Amber is a punctuation mark, never a sentence. Lavender is a whisper, used for hierarchy on dark surfaces." This is the level of brand thinking I bring to every project, every day.
