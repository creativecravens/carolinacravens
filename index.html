01 Case study

Legacy Housing Corporation.

Digital Designer at a Nasdaq-listed manufactured housing company. As the design and web point of contact for sales, engineering, and leadership, I keep handoffs few and decisions fast.

  • 01Private lending launch. Designed the Hard Money Lending page for desktop and mobile, built it as ready-to-code HTML with the full application form, wrote the accompanying brief, and packaged the lot for the web developer with compliance copy cleared through legal.
  • 02Catalog redesign, end to end. Led the full redesign and delivered it with every floor plan complete and correctly placed, carrying it solo through my manager's leave with written daily progress reports to leadership.
  • 0365-icon library. One consistent visual language across print and digital.
  • 04In-house photo + AI. On-site plant photography at Fort Worth plus Firefly, Midjourney and Higgsfield for on-brand imagery, without outside vendors.
  • 05Approval system. A 39-question sign-off document that gets sales and engineering to decisions in one pass instead of another review round.
  • 06Trade shows, brand video, competitive tracking. Full marketing packages for the Disaster Expo and SECO Conference, the production brief and fact-checked script for the board-level brand video, and a live 30-competitor market tracker.
  • 07Published print ad. A two-page spread in the Manufactured Housing Association of Oklahoma's 2026-2027 Membership Directory, with every photo edited by me. See it ↓

Companies don't need more mockups. They need a designer who understands the business, works across teams, and ships. I've designed for global luxury houses like LVMH, L'Oréal, Signet Jewelers, and IWC North America, inside a Nasdaq-listed company, and for small businesses where every dollar has to show.

I use Claude, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, and Higgsfield AI every day to explore and produce faster, then make the call a model can't: what's right for the brand, the customer, and the business.

I trained as a painter first, winning international youth art medals in Osaka, Japan, so taste isn't a buzzword for me. I design in Adobe Creative Cloud, build in HTML, CSS, and real-time 3D, and I'm fluent in English and Spanish.
