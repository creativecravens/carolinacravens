Where it starts

Before the software, there was paint.

I did not arrive at design through a job. I arrived through painting. As a child in Costa Rica I won gold, silver and bronze at the International Children's Art Exhibition in Japan, placed three times in the national painting competition, and showed a solo exhibition at Club Unión in San José. The fine arts degree came later.

University came next: graphic and web design. I learned to build a page by hand, line by line, years before generative AI existed. That grounding is why the AI work holds up now. When these tools arrived I already knew what good looked like and how a site is actually put together, so I never had to take an output on trust. I could direct it.

Nine professional years later, the tools have changed completely. The judgement has not.
