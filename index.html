Who I Am

Taste you can't automate.
Speed you can measure.

AI can generate a hundred options in a minute. Knowing which one is right for the brand, the user, and the business is still a human job, and it's mine. I've spent 8+ years training that eye on brands that operate at the highest level, including LVMH, L'Oréal, Signet Jewelers, and IWC North America, so I know what premium looks like and how to deliver it consistently.

I work on both sides of the market. Inside corporate teams, I ship under legal review and stakeholder sign-off, most recently at Nasdaq-listed Legacy Housing. As a freelancer and studio co-founder, I've built brands and websites for small businesses in Dallas and Costa Rica, where every dollar has to show. That range makes me useful on day one.

I love technology and I'm not afraid of complex tools. I design in Adobe and AI tools, then hand-code in HTML, CSS, and real-time 3D WebGL when a project needs more than a template. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish, so my work connects across U.S. and Latin American audiences without losing brand integrity.
