Companies don't need more mockups. They need a designer who understands the business, works across teams, and ships. I've designed for global luxury houses like LVMH, L'Oréal, Signet Jewelers, and IWC North America, inside a Nasdaq-listed company, and for small businesses where every dollar has to show.

I use Claude, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, and Higgsfield AI every day to explore and produce faster, then make the call a model can't: what's right for the brand, the customer, and the business.

I trained as a painter first, winning international youth art medals in Osaka, Japan, so taste isn't a buzzword for me. I design in Adobe Creative Cloud, build in HTML, CSS, and real-time 3D, and I'm fluent in English and Spanish.
