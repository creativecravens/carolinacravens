Who I Am

A digital designer built for how companies work now.

Companies don't need more mockups. They need a designer who understands the business, works across teams, and ships. I've designed for global luxury houses like LVMH, L'Oréal, Signet Jewelers, and IWC North America, inside a Nasdaq-listed company, and for small businesses where every dollar has to show.

AI makes options cheap. Taste decides which one ships. I use Claude, Adobe Firefly, Midjourney, and Higgsfield AI every day to explore and produce faster, then make the call a model can't: what's right for the brand, the customer, and the business.

I love technology and I'm at home in complex tools. I design in Adobe Creative Cloud, build in HTML, CSS, and real-time 3D WebGL, and work in Jira, Wrike, Monday, and Asana alongside marketing, sales, engineering, and leadership. I'm fluent in English and Spanish.
