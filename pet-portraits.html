Questions

The things people ask before they send one.

What photo should I send?

A clear phone photo is enough. Good light, your dog facing the camera, and nothing covering the face. You do not need a professional camera, and you do not need to get your dog to sit still for a studio.

How much does it cost?

$15 per portrait. One flat price, no upsells, no subscription, no print you have to buy.

What do I actually receive?

A full resolution digital file, sized so you can print it or post it. It is yours to use however you like, on a card, a mug, a frame or Instagram.

How fast will I get it?

Message or text me and I will tell you the current turnaround. December weeks fill up, so earlier is safer if you want it before Christmas.

Can you do cats or other pets?

Yes. Cats, rabbits, horses, birds. Send the photo and ask.

Can I ask for a specific scene?

Yes. Tell me what you have in mind, whether that is tree lights, a red bow, snow, or something of your own. If I cannot do it I will tell you before you pay.

Do I have to be in Dallas?

No. Everything happens over email or text, so it works anywhere in the country. I am based in Dallas-Fort Worth, Texas.
