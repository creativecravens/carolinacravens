Designing with AI · 0:09
Claude Strategy · copy Firefly · Midjourney Higgsfield AI Video

Created by Carolina Cravens with AI tools.

When first drafts become cheap, the value moves to judgment. Designers are not being replaced. The ones who will not adapt are.

I am not a prompt engineer. I am a designer with AI fluency built in, using these tools daily inside a Nasdaq-listed company's brand and legal review, and across client work. AI cannot read a room, challenge a bad brief, or own the outcome when brand, product and legal all want something different. That is the job, and it does not automate.

AI-assisted prototyping

Pages, forms and user flows prototyped for desktop and mobile in a fraction of the time, then refined by hand with clear developer notes.

Rapid concept development

Dozens of on-brand directions in the time it took to rough out three, so stakeholders see real options instead of mood boards.

Campaign and video variation

Consistent variations across formats, markets and channels, including AI video, without a reshoot for every size.

Prompt-system design

Reusable prompt frameworks built around a brand's visual language, so output stays on brand instead of generic.

How we'll work together

Not a prompt engineer. A designer fluent in AI.

Claude is my main collaborator. I make the decisions, and Claude helps me ship them faster and with fewer mistakes. You see drafts early, and most projects take 1 to 3 weeks.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery call

    You + me

    We talk through your business, your customers, and what the site needs to do.

    Human
  2. Step 02

    Strategy and design

    100% human

    I define the audience, the message, and the look. Claude does not decide your brand.

    Human
  3. Step 03

    Build and test

    Claude-assisted

    Claude helps me write and test HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, check pages on phone and desktop, and fix performance problems.

    Claude + Human
  4. Step 04

    Speed, words, and search

    Claude-assisted

    Compressed images and video, bilingual copy, page titles, meta descriptions, structured data, and accessibility checks.

    Claude + Human
  5. Step 05

    Final call, launch, and support

    100% human

    I review and approve every page. Your site goes live with a walkthrough, Google setup, and 30 days of support.

About

A painter first. A designer now.

I painted before I could write. I won gold, silver, and bronze medals at an international youth art competition in Osaka, Japan, and earned top placements in Costa Rica's national painting competition. I also held a solo exhibition at Club Unión in San José. See the atelier.

I grew up in San José, Costa Rica, and studied at The Art Institute of Dallas, where I earned my Bachelor of Fine Arts in 2020. Today I work in Dallas-Fort Worth. I speak English and Spanish fluently, so I design for U.S. and Latin American audiences in both languages.

That mix is the point. The art taught me to see. The web taught me to ship. AI lets me do both faster, without giving up the taste.
