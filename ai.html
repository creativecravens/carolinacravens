Why This Page Exists

AI is the most
requested design skill.

Design skill is now the single most requested skill in AI-related job postings, ahead of coding and cloud skills. Most hiring managers say their need for designers has gone up or held steady over the past year, and a sizable share plan to open more design roles in the months ahead. Designers aren't being replaced. The ones who won't adapt are.

My approach isn't "prompt engineer." It's brand strategist and creative director first, with AI fluency built in as a visible, named skill, not a replacement for the craft. I use Firefly, Midjourney, Higgsfield AI, and DALL·E daily at Legacy Housing and across freelance work to move through concepting, iteration, and production faster, while every brand decision, every "does this fit the business" call, stays with me.

AI can produce options. It can't read a room, challenge a bad brief, or own the outcome when brand, product, and legal all want something different. That's the job. That's what doesn't automate.

Since 2026, I've been the AI-Powered Digital Designer at Legacy Housing Corporation (Nasdaq: LEGH), a publicly traded manufactured housing company. This is the current, active proof of concept for everything on this page: I use Firefly, Midjourney, and Higgsfield AI to produce on-brand marketing imagery and ad variations for digital and print campaigns, speed up the product catalog's visual production, and generate concept work for expos, all while keeping brand strategy and final sign-off entirely in my hands. This isn't a hypothetical future skill set. It's what I'm doing at my current job, right now.
