AI is changing how design work gets done, but it isn't replacing the people who decide what good looks like. When first drafts become cheap, the value moves to judgment, taste, and knowing which option actually works for the brand and the user. Designers aren't being replaced. The ones who won't adapt are.

My approach isn't "prompt engineer." It's designer first, with AI fluency built in. I use Claude, Firefly, Midjourney, Higgsfield AI, and DALL·E every day at Legacy Housing and across freelance work to move through prototyping, iteration, and production faster, while every design decision stays with me.

AI can produce options. It can't read a room, challenge a bad brief, or own the outcome when brand, product, and legal all want something different. That's the job, and that's what doesn't automate.

Process

Where AI fits and where it doesn't.

AI sits in the middle of my process, never at the start or the end. The brief and the final call are always human.

  1. Step 01

    Brief, users & strategy

    100% human

    Understand the business, the audience, and what success looks like before anything gets made.

    Human
  2. Step 02

    Prototyping & exploration

    AI-assisted iteration

    Dozens of directions explored fast, then narrowed down with design judgment.

    AI + Human
  3. Step 03

    Production & polish

    AI-accelerated

    On-brand imagery, variations, and layouts produced quickly, then refined by hand.

    AI + Human
  4. Step 04

    Final review & handoff

    100% human

    Every detail checked against the brand, the brief, and legal before it ships.
